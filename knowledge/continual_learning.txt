Continual learning is the ability of an AI system to learn from new data or tasks over time while retaining previously acquired knowledge.

A major challenge in continual learning is catastrophic forgetting. Catastrophic forgetting occurs when learning new information causes a model to lose previously learned knowledge.

Another challenge is the stability-plasticity trade-off. Plasticity allows a model to learn new information, while stability helps the model preserve previously learned knowledge.

Data efficiency is also important because continual learning systems may have limited access to previous training data. Models therefore need to learn effectively from relatively small amounts of new data.

Distribution shift is another challenge. The distribution of new data may differ from previous data, causing the model to perform poorly on earlier or new tasks.

Continual learning in AI agents can also involve safety problems. An adaptive agent should not learn harmful or unreliable behaviors from new interactions.

Evaluation is difficult because a continual learning system must be evaluated not only on its newest task but also on its ability to retain knowledge from previous tasks.
