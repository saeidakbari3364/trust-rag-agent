Quantum computing for AI agents may introduce challenges related to computational complexity, limited quantum hardware, noise, error correction, and the difficulty of integrating quantum algorithms with existing AI systems.

Quantum hardware is currently limited by noise and errors. AI agents that depend on quantum computation may therefore need robust methods for handling unreliable quantum outputs.

Another challenge is the integration of quantum algorithms with classical AI systems. Hybrid quantum-classical architectures may require new methods for coordinating classical and quantum computation.

Evaluation is also challenging because quantum AI systems may require different benchmarks and evaluation methods compared with conventional AI systems.
